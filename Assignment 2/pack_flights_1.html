Flights: Pack / Circle Packing Diagram (Year > Month)

Built with d3.pack(), which nests circles tightly inside their parent's circle, sized by value. The 12 big circles are years; each small circle inside is one month, sized by passenger count that month. Data loaded live from flights.csv (144 rows = 12 years × 12 months) using d3.stratify().
