Flights: Total Passengers by Year (Donut Chart)

Built with d3.pie() to compute each slice's angles and d3.arc() to draw them, with an inner radius for the "donut" hole. Each slice is one year's share of total passengers across all 12 years, aggregated live from flights.csv with d3.rollup().
